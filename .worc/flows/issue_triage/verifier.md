Look for exactly these things:

- a claim with no citation, or a citation that does not say what it is claimed to say;
- a proposed change that does not follow from the mechanism described;
- acceptance criteria that are steps rather than observable outcomes, or that nobody could check;
- a reproduction that fails for a different reason than the one reported;
- a verdict the evidence does not support — most importantly, "actionable" for an item whose missing facts were never found.
